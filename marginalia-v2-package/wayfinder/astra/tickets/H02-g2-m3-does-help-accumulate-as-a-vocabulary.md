## Question
Whitepaper line 13 says help accumulates. Is the vocabulary a first-class feature, and what earns an entry: every accepted definition, only ones the reader keeps, or an explicit "remember this word"?


## Fable's recommendation
Explicit keep only. A definition the reader kept is a note; a word they asked to remember is vocabulary. Automatic accumulation makes the margin talk about things the reader did not choose.
