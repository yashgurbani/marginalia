# Vocabulary scope decision

status: resolved 2026-09-18 by Yash
type: grilling, Yash
blocked by: none

## Question
The whitepaper gives vocabulary entries three origins: used, looked up, skip. It describes gathering them from notes and lookups. Decision H02 (Astra map) chose an explicit Remember action only, and that is what ships.

1. Does explicit-only stay as the product, or does gathering from the reader's own notes return, locally and with no send?
2. Whichever holds, each saved term can show its origin beside it. Is that wanted now?
3. Related open ticket: Automatic definitions under a site grant (H08, Astra map).

## Resolution
Yash, 2026-09-18: "and also over time as usage goes on and knowledge base is built".

Decision: explicit Remember stays, and vocabulary also gathers over time from the reader's own use (notes and lookups), as the whitepaper describes. Gathering is local and sends nothing. Each term shows its origin: used, looked up, or skip. A new build ticket is needed: "Vocabulary gathers from use".
