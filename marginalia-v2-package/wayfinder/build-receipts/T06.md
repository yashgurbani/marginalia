## HTTP integration contract

- `GET /api/jobs?thread=` → `{ configured, available, unavailableReason?, jobs }`
- `POST /api/jobs/prepare` → host-prepared T13 consent preview plus the non-authoritative host-derived job plan to submit after approval
- `POST /api/consent/decision` → fresh grant from a trusted paired surface
- `POST /api/jobs` → persisted job/attempt, only after matching preparation + grant
- `GET /api/jobs/:jobId`
- `POST /api/jobs/:jobId/cancel`
- `POST /api/jobs/:jobId/prepare-retry`, then `/retry`
- `POST /api/jobs/:jobId/prepare-followup`, then `/followups`
- `GET /api/consent/settings`, `POST /api/consent/settings`
- `GET /api/replies?threadId=` returns active versions and real tombstones (`includeRemoved=true`) in `{ replies, source, views }`
- `GET /api/reply-view`, `POST /api/reply-view`, `POST /api/reply-check`
- `GET|POST /api/settings/models`, `GET /api/vocabulary`, `POST /api/vocabulary/delete`

New Ask, retry, and follow-up each require their own preparation and permission binding. Client-provided model/provider/policy/capabilities are not authoritative. The model comes from T11’s frozen `ModelSelection`; its settings revision and compatibility key join T13’s permission fingerprint for continuation reuse. A mismatch forks instead of resuming.
