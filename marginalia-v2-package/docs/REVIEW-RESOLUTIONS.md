## Documentation and research corrections to carry forward

- Distinguish reopenable saved work from automatic second-document reuse until both work.
- Credit explorable explanations, Tangle and Idyll alongside annotation and scientific-reader prior art. Compare the combined workflow without universal novelty claims.
- Research on desirable difficulty and expertise reversal motivates tests; it does not establish an optimal difficulty for adult web reading or prove that a particular collapse control harms experts.
- Add research on multiple representations and ICAP when revising the public whitepaper. Slider activity alone is not evidence of learning. Avoid compulsory quizzes.
- Keep numerical validity, source correspondence, interpretation and reader usefulness separate in the UI and evaluation.
- Reconcile weekly model-fitting language with the small alpha's limits; audit confident errors separately from refusals and from kept replies.
- Verify actual dependency licenses at adoption. Pro flags elkjs as non-MIT; use verified dagre metadata if it meets the current diagram need.
- Verify current Codex embedding/authentication documentation and Node release dates before revising claims. The existing policy/date assertions are not acceptance evidence.
- Keep the scalar growth model distinct from the kinematic vortex field. The latter is divergence-free but, for constant parameters, does not demonstrate growing vorticity or singularity formation.
