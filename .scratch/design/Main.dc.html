§1 The scattering problem
full

It is well known that the α and β particles suffer deflexions from their rectilinear paths by encounters with atoms of matter. This scattering is far more marked for the β than for the α particle on account of the much smaller momentum and energy of the former particle.

There seems to be no doubt that such swiftly moving particles pass through the atoms in their path, and that the deflexions observed are due to the strong electric field traversed within the atomic system.

§2 The theory of single scattering
full I know thisLost me

Consider an atom which contains a charge ±Ne at its centre surrounded by a sphere of electrification containing a charge ∓Ne supposed uniformly distributed throughout a sphere of radius R.

The electric force X and the potential V at a distance r from the centre of an atom for a point inside the atom are given by X = Ne(1/r² − r/R³), V = Ne(1/r − 3/2R + r²/2R³).
